## Cap. 161 / MCHK — advertising vs education

Escalate to counsel when:

- Education copy starts to look like canvassing for a named practitioner or clinic
(addresses, phones, booking, fees, WhatsApp, practice-site links, comparative
doctor claims, testimonials, before/after success rates).
- Reviewer identity is shown next to promotion or referral language.
- Distribution channels (QR packs, social, email) start bundling clinic contact
with education pages.

Education goal remains: symptom recognition, when to seek care, classes of
options a doctor may discuss — without booking or referral.

## Cap. 231 s.3 — audit watch areas

Internal audit reminders (not a legal clearance):

1. **Treatment claims** — avoid wording that would likely lead a reader to use a
   named medicine, appliance or therapy for Schedule conditions; keep class / INN
   education and “doctor decides” framing.
2. **Comparative visuals** — simulators and tables are illustrations, not outcome
   comparisons between clinics or products.
3. **Metadata / OG / structured data** — titles and descriptions must stay
   education-framed; no clinic shopfront signals.
4. **Clinic-linked distribution** — QR `/qr` stays off the home/tools list;
   do not pair education QR packs with practice contact cards.
